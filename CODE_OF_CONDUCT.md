# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We expect project participants to communicate respectfully, focus on the work, and assume good intent while being clear about technical concerns.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sustained disruption, or publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates these standards.

Report conduct concerns to conduct@chaterface.com. Reports will be reviewed privately and handled according to the severity and context of the incident.
